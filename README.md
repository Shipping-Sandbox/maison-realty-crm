# Maison

A CRM for elite real estate professionals.

**Live:** https://real-estate-crm.bolt.host

## What it is

Maison is a CRM made for real estate agents and agencies who work at the premium end of the market. It keeps the whole sales workflow in one place, from the first enquiry to a closed deal.

## What it covers

- **Listings:** keep your properties organised
- **Clients:** track buyers, sellers and their history
- **Deals:** follow each deal through the pipeline
- **Appointments:** schedule viewings and meetings

## Who it is for

Independent agents, brokers and boutique agencies that want a polished, focused tool rather than a generic sales CRM.
